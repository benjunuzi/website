How to play: Move your mouse or tap to highlight a receiver, then press Space, click/tap the field, or press THROW. After the catch, run with Arrow Keys / WASD or the on-screen pad. Reach the end zone for a touchdown. Press STIFF ARM while running to shove a nearby defender. You have four downs per drive.
