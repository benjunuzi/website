# Sports Game Ideas

Brainstorm batch for the Sports category on ben.junuzi.com.
Each idea lists the hook (what the player does), a Game Lab build note
(Catch It / Dodge It / Tap Attack / custom), and a suggested emoji + colour theme.

## Field & Court

1. **Penalty Shootout Pro** — Pick your corner, charge the power bar, and beat a diving keeper. 5 shots, sudden death if tied.
   - Build: Tap Attack (aim + power). Emoji ⚽. Theme green.
2. **Volleyball Rally** — Keep the ball off the sand; every clean hit speeds the rally up until someone spikes past you.
   - Build: custom paddle-style. Emoji 🏐. Theme orange.
3. **Buzzer Beater** — Bounce passes, dodge defenders, and sink one shot before the clock hits zero.
   - Build: Tap Attack with moving targets. Emoji 🏀. Theme orange.
4. **Handball Hurl** — Wind up and hurl the ball into the top corner while the goalie predicts your pattern.
   - Build: custom aim/timing. Emoji 🤾. Theme blue.
5. **Badminton Blitz** — Fast rallies, a shuttlecock that curves, and a "perfect smash" window on every high shot.
   - Build: custom paddle. Emoji 🏸. Theme green.
6. **Rugby Ruck** — Ram through tacklers, offload before you're dragged down, and touch it down past the line.
   - Build: custom runner. Emoji 🏉. Theme purple.
7. **Cricket Slog Fest** — Time your swing against spin, pace, and yorkers; clear the ropes for a six.
   - Build: Tap Attack timing. Emoji 🏏. Theme green.
8. **Ultimate Frisbee** — Float a curving disc around defenders into the end zone.
   - Build: custom aim/curve. Emoji 🥏. Theme blue.
